**NOVA DRIFT** — ride a comet through a starfield, catch gold shards to build a combo, dodge the void rocks, and fire a nova burst to clear the way. Reach 500 points and the constellation is yours. A complete offline browser game: title, play, pause, win and game-over screens, a best score that persists, synthesized WebAudio sound, and no network requests at all.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Or open `game/index.html` directly in a desktop browser — the game is plain static files.

## How to play

- **Steer** with ← ↑ ↓ → / WASD, or guide the comet with the mouse.
- **Catch gold shards**: each one scores 10 × your combo multiplier. Chain pickups within 2.5 s to raise the combo (up to ×5).
- **Dodge the void rocks**: a hit costs one of your 3 lives and breaks the combo.
- **SPACE or click** fires a **NOVA burst**: shatters nearby rocks and gathers nearby shards. 3 charges, they recharge over time.
- **Win** at 500 points (STARBORN!), then keep drifting in endless mode or play again. **P** pauses, **M** mutes, **R** restarts.
